# Return Policy

Source: ToolHoo return policy page (staging.toolhoo.com/return-policy)

## How a return works
1. Submit a request: click the "Submit a request" button on the Return Policy page and fill out the form.
2. Receive return authorization: you receive a return authorization (RA) form with the exact return address and a shipping label. This may take 24-72 hours.
3. Ship it back: print and attach the provided UPS shipping label as well as the RA form to the outside of the box.
4. Receive refund: once the merchandise is received and confirmed to be in satisfactory condition, your refund will be issued.

## What can I return?
If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, ToolHoo will accept a return for a refund or exchange on products in new or unused condition within 7 days of delivery.

Items must be in new condition and in the original packaging. Do not assemble, install or modify the product in any way. In the event of a fitment problem, flaw or defect, pictures may be necessary to complete your request.

The product must include the original tags, user manual, warranty cards, freebies and accessories. The few exceptions for items that cannot be returned include items marked "Non-Returnable". This is clearly indicated on the product page before placing your order.

Note: it is important to write the order number and the return tracking number on your return package to avoid inconvenience or delay in processing your return.

## Rejected returns
If your returned item does not meet the above requirements, ToolHoo reserves the right to reject any request for a refund. If your return request has been rejected, the item will be delivered back to you between 4 and 6 days. The item will be sent to scrap after three failed delivery attempts and no refund will be given.

## What are my shipping fees?
For orders delivered within Pakistan where the reason for return is not a vendor error, the cost of the initial shipping charge and a return fee will be deducted from your refund. A prepaid shipping label will be provided.

Freight, international and Pakistan returns are accepted. However, coordination of return shipping is the responsibility of the client. If ToolHoo makes an error, a return shipping label will be provided.
