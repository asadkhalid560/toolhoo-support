# Payment Methods

> SAMPLE POLICY for the demo store. Replace this text with the real ToolHoo policy.

## Accepted payments
Cash on delivery, bank transfer, and major debit and credit cards are accepted.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available on most orders. Large orders may need an advance payment.

## Invoices
A tax invoice is available on request. Share the business name and tax number when you order.

## Failed or duplicate payments
If you were charged twice or a payment failed but money left your account, contact support
with the transaction reference. Duplicate charges are refunded within 7 working days.
