# Shipping and Delivery

> SAMPLE POLICY for the demo store. Replace this text with the real ToolHoo policy.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Standard delivery takes 2 to 5 working days inside the country.
Remote areas can take up to 8 working days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders above a minimum order value shown at checkout.
Below that value a flat shipping fee applies.

## Order tracking
A tracking number is sent by email and SMS once the order is dispatched.

## Out of stock items
If an item is out of stock, the product page shows it as unavailable. Contact support to ask
about restocking dates or to request a similar item.
