# Choosing a Drill Bit

General guidance for choosing twist drills. Always check the exact specification on the product page.

## Material of the drill
- HSS (high speed steel): the general purpose choice for mild steel, aluminium, wood and plastic.
- HSS-Co (cobalt, for example M35): holds its edge at higher temperatures, a better choice for stainless steel and hard alloys.
- Solid carbide: very hard and rigid, used for hard materials and production work, but brittle, so it needs a rigid machine setup.

## Coatings
- Bright finish: cheapest, fine for soft materials and occasional use.
- Black oxide: reduces friction and heat a little and lasts longer than bright finish.
- Gold TiN coating: harder surface and longer life in steel, helps reduce wear.

## Point angle
- 118 degrees: general purpose, good for soft materials and wood.
- 135 degrees: self centering split point, better for harder materials such as steel and stainless steel.

## Drilling tips
- Use slower speeds for harder materials and larger diameters.
- Use cutting fluid on steel and stainless steel to control heat.
- Start with a center punch mark, or a short drill, so the bit does not wander.
- Clear chips often when drilling deep holes.
