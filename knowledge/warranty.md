# Warranty

> SAMPLE POLICY for the demo store. Replace this text with the real ToolHoo policy.

## What is covered
Products are covered against manufacturing defects for 6 months from the delivery date.
Cutting tools are covered against defects in material and finish, not against normal wear.

## What is not covered
Wear from normal use, breakage from incorrect speed or feed, use on unsuitable material,
overheating, and damage from misuse or accidents.

## Making a claim
Contact support with your order number, a photo of the defect, and a short description of how
the tool was used. Approved claims are repaired, replaced or refunded.
