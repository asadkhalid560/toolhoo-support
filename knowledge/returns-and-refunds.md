# Returns and Refunds

> SAMPLE POLICY for the demo store. Replace this text with the real ToolHoo policy.

## Return window
Unused tools in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
Drill bits, taps and other cutting tools that have been used or resharpened cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
1. Contact support with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. Wait for the return approval and the return address.
3. Pack the item in its original packaging and ship it back.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days after the returned item
passes inspection. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged
or the wrong item was sent.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report a damaged or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the
packaging. We will replace it or refund it in full, including shipping.
